# What is known about the OpenAI round, and how sure we are

## Well supported (several independent sources)
- OpenAI coding is practical and production-style, not LeetCode.
- Problems are staged ("gates") and extended as requirements change.
- Messy-code refactoring with tests staying green is a known format.
- Edge cases, tests and communication matter, and a working solution comes first.

## Conflicting or uncertain
- Whether AI is allowed. Some guides say AI is strictly prohibited in standard rounds; others (an FDE candidate report and an Applied AI guide) say AI is permitted or the round is "AI-enabled". A beta agentic round is described in one guide. Your recruiter calls it AI-assisted, so prepare for AI use but confirm the policy.
- The agentic-build focus for the Applied AI role comes from you. I did not find a source that confirms it for OpenAI.
- No source is a first-hand report of this exact round.
