## Sources

The lessons are the author's own, written for the integration design coverage plan on 17 November 2026. They work one case end to end: Stanway Outdoor, a fictional mid-size retailer, syncing products, stock, orders and shipping with an unnamed online marketplace and its own back-office systems. Systems are named by responsibility, not by vendor. Each lesson lists its primary sources in its own References section. Each lesson opens on a composite engagement, labelled in its Limits section; customers, names and figures are illustrative.
