## Sources

Each lesson lists its primary sources (standards, specifications and engineering references) in its own References section. Each teaching lesson opens with a composite engagement: the customers, staff and figures are illustrative, not real customers. The practice page's customers are fictional. The lessons describe CRM, ERP and ticketing systems generically and name no vendor product.
