## Sources

The lessons are the author's own, written from general hiring practice and experience writing and reviewing take-homes. They describe no specific company's rubric. Each lesson lists its primary sources in its own References section. Customers, names and figures are fictional and labelled as illustrations.

## Editing

Edit these files, not the generated copies under `site/content/learn/`, then run `npm run sync` from `site/`. The site slug is fixed once the lesson is published.
