    How to turn this repository into a structured learning product for Applied AI and Forward Deployed Engineering, built on what the repo already contains and honest about what it lacks.

    01Executive summary

    The repository is already a strong judgment-layer course: about 430 markdown files covering system design, RAG, agents, production trade-offs, delivery, behavioural answers and last-week revision. The site (site/) publishes only 85 of those pages, all practice, case-study or revision material. The explanatory curriculum in 01_Concept_Tutorials/ and 05_FDE_Delivery_and_Scoping/ is not on the site at all.

    The recommendation is to keep the name Forward Deployed and add a curriculum layer above the existing pages rather than rebuild:

    1. Ten tracks (T0–T9). Six are mandatory for the core paths, and every module points at one canonical source file.
    2. Six navigation areas: Start, Learn, Design, Practice, Interview and Progress.
    3. A progress model that extends today's fd:v1:* localStorage keys with attempts, confidence and a revision queue. Accounts come later.

    Learning content and interview practice stay separate: interview pages link to curriculum modules instead of restating them.

    About 70% of the MVP can be built from existing or reorganized content. The real gaps are:

    • engineering foundations (Python, SQL, distributed systems, coding patterns);
    • ML and LLM internals;
    • model adaptation and serving beyond one case pack each;
    • mock interviews;
    • projects.

    The mock interviews, incident drills and red-team fixtures you deleted on 4 Oct are still in git history. Restoring them selectively is the fastest way to fill two of those gaps.

    Revision 2 adds a full read of the reference PDFs, study plans and company packs (§3, "Deep read"). The company packs hold a lot of generic material: 12 code-review drills, an incident-triage method, exec-communication guides, 23 decomposition cases and 9 tested resilience solutions. The blueprint moves those into the core modules, keeps company pages as thin "Company Pack" entries that link to them, and adopts the study plans' tier, hours and company add-on model for learning paths.

    05Recommended website name

    Forward Deployed

    Tagline: Applied AI engineering, from the customer's problem to production, and the interview that tests both.

    Why keep it EXISTING

    • The phrase names the job. It means engineering at the customer, on production systems, under ambiguity. Applied AI roles at model labs and platform companies increasingly carry that title, and it signals production and real-world work more directly than any "AI course" name.
    • It's already the brand everywhere. It's the current SITE_NAME (site/src/lib/editorial.ts:93), the Vercel project (forward-deployed) and the production domain. A rename costs links and recognition and adds nothing a sharper tagline can't.

    The weakness: software and data engineers moving into AI may read it as niche. Fix that with the tagline and an "Applied AI Engineering" descriptor in the header, not with a new name.

    07Product positioning

    Positioning statement. For engineers moving into Applied AI and forward-deployed roles, Forward Deployed is the structured course that teaches how to take an AI system from an ambiguous customer problem to a defended production design, and rehearses every step under interview conditions. Generic AI courses stop at the model or the demo. This one trains the judgment around them: decomposition, trade-offs, delivery and the spoken answer.

    Difficulty levels

    Keep the three levels already in editorial.ts (Foundation | Intermediate | Advanced) and add one: Interview-ready. It applies to timed practice and mocks and is earned through assessment, never self-declared. REORGANIZED

    09Website information architecture

    There are six top-level areas. Each one answers a single learner question:

    • Start: where do I begin?
    • Learn: what must I know?
    • Design: what does a good answer look like?
    • Practice: can I do it myself?
    • Interview: can I do it under pressure?
    • Progress: where am I?

    Reference material sits in a utility bar (Glossary, Library, Search) rather than taking a top-level slot.

    Deliberately left out

    • Community and discussion: deferred to the long term (§21). A static, single-author site with no accounts can't moderate a forum.
    • Administration and content management: git is the CMS. Authoring stays in root markdown, then npm run sync, then check:content, then a PR and the Vercel preview. An in-app editor would duplicate that pipeline and bypass the content contract.

    12Dependency-aware learning paths

    Each path flags every module as required, recommended or optional, the tier model from 09_Study_Plans/. Company variants follow the same plans' "shared core plus add-ons" structure: the General core covers 98% of the OpenAI/Anthropic tier-1 hours and 76% of Google's. Hour estimates are calibrated against those plans: FDE path about 140–180 hours, revision about 55 hours, coding about 1 hour a day alongside. REORGANIZED

    Gating is soft: modules never lock. Instead a prerequisite check card appears ("You haven't passed T2.4's checkpoint; this module assumes it"), and only checkpoints and mocks count towards readiness.

    Hard locks suit cohort products. This is self-directed, and experienced learners need to skip freely. NEW

    16Progress-tracking system

    Levels and statuses

    Eight statuses: the seven from the brief plus Skipped (from the study-plan workbooks). They map onto today's three so existing saved progress carries over:

    • not-started becomes Not started.
    • practiced becomes In progress.
    • mastered becomes Completed.

    Mastered is new and stricter: it means passed twice, at least 7 days apart. Blocked means a prerequisite checkpoint was failed. Ready for review means a project was submitted for self or peer review. Needs revision is set automatically when confidence decays or a checkpoint is failed. Skipped marks a module tested out of or deliberately excluded; it counts as done for the path but adds no readiness evidence. REORGANIZED

    18Content model

    Instructional content stays in root markdown, in the folder for its kind. The model lives in a new sidecar, site/src/lib/curriculum.ts, alongside editorial.ts and like it free of runtime imports.

    • The sidecar maps tracks, sections and modules onto existing page references (trackId/slug, or a lesson file path). Reuse happens by reference, never by copying.
    • Existing EditorialMeta becomes the metadata block shared by Lesson, Exercise and Case study.
    • Existing REVIEWS[].learnFirst/goDeeper becomes the Module relationship fields.
