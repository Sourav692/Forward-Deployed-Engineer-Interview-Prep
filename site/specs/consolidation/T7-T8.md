## Source conflicts

- The five short practice mocks are word-for-word identical apart from the scenario name. Their narrowing question quotes "all company knowledge in chat" even in the SRE and compliance mocks. The scenario lessons keep the five questions and reword that one per case.
- The full mocks share most of their transcript. Some shared lines fit only the knowledge-assistant case, for example "I would not index every possible source" in the SRE mock and the weak candidate asking "which documents they want to upload" in every mock. Shared lines are taught once in lesson 1, worded generically; case-specific lines stay in the scenario lessons.
- Every full mock scores the strong candidate 4.5 out of 5, the weak 2 and the average 3, with the same debrief.
- The full-mock README calls the short mocks "shorter 45-minute versions", but both rounds state a 45-minute duration.
- The Telling the Story sources refer to handbook modules and worked narratives that are kept privately (insurer and lender flows). The lessons name them without linking, and the implementation-flow worked example is written as a template rather than inventing those projects' details.
