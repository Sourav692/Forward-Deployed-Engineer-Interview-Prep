## Source conflicts

Each conflict below is stated in the lesson that uses it.

- **Faithfulness thresholds.** Module 1 alerts below 0.75 and reviews below 0.8. Module 2 gates on faithfulness 0.80, answer relevancy 0.75 and context recall 0.70.
- **Evaluation set size.** One source says 100 to 200 questions; another says 100 to 500 pairs.
- **Chunk overlap and units.** The sources give 10 to 15% or 10 to 20% overlap, and measure chunks in tokens or in characters.
- **Vector-store scale thresholds.**
  - One source says pgvector is enough below tens of millions of vectors.
  - Managed stores are suggested under about 50M vectors in one source, and called the practical choice at 10M+ in another.
  - HNSW is suggested up to about 10M vectors and IVF-PQ beyond about 100M.
- **Quantization savings.** One source says 8 to 32 times less memory; another works an example to about 30 times.
- **RRF formula.** One source writes `1/(k+rank)` and another `1/(k+rank+1)`; they are the same formula with ranks counted from zero.
- **Merging hybrid results.** The Handbook, the retrieval tutorial and the strategies tutorial merge by rank. The advanced techniques tutorial blends min-max-normalised scores with `alpha`.
- **Default reranker.** The advanced techniques tutorial limits LLM reranking to low volume. The Handbook platform uses a batched LLM reranker by default.
- **LangGraph per-request context.** The LangGraph tutorial's self-check allows `configurable`, but its gotcha reserves it for the checkpointer. The lesson follows the gotcha.
- **LangChain API generations.** Module 2 uses `ConversationBufferMemory` and `.get_relevant_documents()`. The LangChain tutorial uses `RunnableWithMessageHistory`, `trim_messages` and `.invoke`.
- **Confirmation handling.** The Handbook's graph keeps running with a `needs_confirmation` tool message, while the from-scratch loop pauses. The Handbook names the pause as the better production shape.

## Glossary

The new lessons spread three capitalised terms onto three or more pages:

- `PQ` (product quantization) and `INT8` (8-bit integer) were added to `GLOSSARY`.
- `TB` was added to `NOT_ACRONYMS` as a unit, and `TD` as the mermaid `flowchart TD` direction keyword.

30 of the 32 source mermaid diagrams were ported into the lessons. Two duplicates were dropped: a second copy of the naive pipeline and the evaluator-optimizer cycle.
