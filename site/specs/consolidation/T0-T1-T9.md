## Source conflicts

- **Canary size (T1.4).** Module 10's CI/CD pipeline example sends 10% of traffic to the canary; its model-upgrade example starts at 5% of users. The lessons keep each figure in its own context.
- **HPA and queue depth (T1.4).** Module 10 says an HPA scales on CPU "and on a custom metric like queue depth", then says KEDA can scale from a queue "which HPA cannot do on its own". Lesson 2 keeps both statements: HPA for CPU-bound tiers, KEDA for queue-driven GPU scaling.
- **Coverage guide staleness (T0.1).** `REPOSITORY_COVERAGE_GUIDE.md` says the site syncs only worksheets, scenario packs, revision and Blitzy material. That predates the Learn section, so the lesson leaves it out rather than repeating it. The guide's `09_Study_Plans/` row indexes a purchased third-party library and is also left out.
