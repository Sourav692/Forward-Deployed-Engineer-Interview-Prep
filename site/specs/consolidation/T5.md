## Source conflicts found

- **Whiteboard time budget.** The method gives entities 8–12, architecture 12–20 and the deep dive 20–40. The scripts' own budgets use architecture 8–15, deep dive 15–35, cross-cutting 35–45 and failure and scale 45–55. Stated in The Six-Step Whiteboard Clock.
- **Step 5 count.** The method says "five sub-sections" but lists six (multi-tenancy, security, observability, evaluation, failure modes, scale). Stated in Deep Dive, Failure Modes, and the Close.
- **Guardrail rules.** The agent-platform script introduces "five ordered rules" but lists seven. Stated in Safe Workflow Automation for Non-Technical Users.
- **Risk mitigations built.** The delivery script's close says two of four mitigations are automatic checks; its own table marks three of four as built. Stated in Delivering an Agent in Two Weeks with Enforced Gates.
- **Cheat-sheet numbering.** The Databricks script numbers two different lines "9."; not reproduced.
