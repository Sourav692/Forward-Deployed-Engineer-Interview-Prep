## Source conflicts found

- **Gate signer name.** The delivery framework README calls the evaluation and rollback signer the "FDE lead"; the theory doc and handbook call it the Forward Deployed Architect (FDA). Flagged in Role-Gated Stage Gates.
- **Risk mitigations built.** `INTERVIEW_SCRIPT.md` shows three of four mitigations built in its risk table but says "two of four" in its closing summary. Lesson uses three and names scope-change control as the gap.
- **Churn recall gain.** The six-stages handbook doc gives +22% recall in the modelling stage and "~25%" in the impact stage. Flagged in Telling a Delivery Story in Six Stages.
- **Booking MVP size.** The scoping guide's Topic 3.3 sizes an MVP at three to five weeks; its booking case study (6.1) sizes a single-location booking MVP at six to ten weeks with more scope. Flagged in Estimates People Can Trust.
- **"Five things" listing six.** The scoping guide's Topic 2.2 heading promises five estimate-inflating factors and lists six. Flagged in The System Behind the Screens.
- **12-to-7 versus under 5.** The guide's pilot report (12 to 7 minutes) does not reach its own example goal of "under 5 minutes". Noted in Proving Business Impact.
