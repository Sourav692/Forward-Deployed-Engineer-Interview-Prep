# Stage 11 Architecture Snapshot

Version: `v0.11-stage-11`. Previous: `v0.10-stage-10`. Decisions: [ADR-011](../../architecture/decisions/ADR-011-residency-aware-regional-cells.md), amended by [ADR-013](../../architecture/decisions/ADR-013-regional-authority-and-route-generations.md).

Retain all runtime and Stage 10 lineage controls. Add trusted tenant placement, EU-primary and EU-secondary for A/B, US-home for C, data-class replication, regional private/provider profiles, recovery eligibility and usage reconciliation. Current policy, audit, the current approved generation, ready artifacts and capacity remain mandatory for promotion. Async result copies may lose acknowledged operation evidence within the stated RPO. The loss bound is last renewal + lease duration − replication watermark, frozen at isolation, because a partitioned live writer may acknowledge until its lease expires. Hold the uncertain interval rather than blindly redispatch.

Residency is enforced at the endpoint. Each residency has its own hostname: the EU host resolves only to EU ingress, and its TLS key exists only there. After sign-in, placement binds a tenant to its host. An ingress that receives a tenant not placed there rejects the request before reading its body. DNS chooses only among ingress points inside one residency; client location never decides where A/B content is decrypted.

Tenant A/B policy, membership, model approval/withdrawal, release-bundle generation, alias bindings, deletion tombstones, current source versions and writer-ownership records live in an EU-only authority store, consensus-replicated across EU-primary, EU-secondary and a small EU witness location. Losing EU-primary leaves a 2-of-3 quorum in the EU. Tenant C has the equivalent store in its US-home cell, and no authority record leaves its tenant's region. Bulk data, indexes and caches replicate asynchronously and are rebuilt from authority. Writer ownership is a quorum lease with an epoch that increases on every grant. Promotion waits for the old lease to expire plus a clock-drift margin, takes a new lease (epoch 8), installs epoch 8 as the minimum accepted epoch in every store the new writer will use (the surviving region's database, object store, job workers and the Model Gateway's billable-usage write path), and confirms each refuses an epoch 7 probe. The isolated region's stores are quarantined, not probed: nothing they accept after isolation becomes authority, and failback rebuilds them. Reachability or a health check is never fencing. If the quorum is lost, nothing is promoted, no protected disclosure is made, and the platform reports authority unavailable. The global control plane holds only opaque placement and is never authority.

Usage evidence is keyed by tenant, operation and attempt: a redelivered attempt counts once, a retry in another region is extra spend, and a conflicting revision of an attempt is rejected. Cost per success divides all attempt spend plus fixed cost by successful workflows.

The executed slices are a pure recovery eligibility/capacity/unit-cost worksheet, which reports safety gates separately from whether the plan meets the RTO, and an in-memory attempt-keyed usage ledger with no epoch field. No cloud region, DNS, network, replication, consensus store, actual lease or epoch enforcement, provider billing integration, or load measurement is claimed. Stage 12 inherits the regional cells, residency hostnames, the EU authority quorum placement and lineage architecture with these explicit production gaps.
