# Stage 12 Architecture Snapshot

Version: `v0.12-stage-12`. Previous: `v0.11-stage-11`. Decision: [ADR-012](../../architecture/decisions/ADR-012-versioned-regional-platform-contracts.md).

Retain the enterprise supplier workflow and all earlier roles: API Gateway, Identity/Policy Service, AI App Service, Metadata DB and history, Queue and Workers, Document Ingestion, Search Index, immutable passage caches, bounded fair admission, audit and Telemetry, Evaluation Service, Model Gateway, proposed GPU Pool, Fine-Tuning Jobs and Model Registry. Retain trusted current resource policy, approved egress, tombstones and source versions, stable scoped operation keys, worker epochs and unknown-outcome reconciliation, version-bound releases and model lineage.

Keep A and B inside EU-primary or eligible EU-secondary and C inside US-home. Maintain one authoritative writer per tenant, verified fencing before regional promotion, each region's [Stage 11](../11-multi-region/guide.md) authority store, current policy, audit and readiness, copy-watermark and RPO checks, and regional request, token and slot capacity. Async operation evidence loss still requires recovery holds, not blind redispatch.

Add versioned app manifests, desired-state validation, a migration metadata controller, supported developer onboarding, an ownership and exception operating model and a decision portfolio. The global control plane validates manifests and publishes signed, versioned route generations under opaque tenant and app IDs; it holds no prompts, documents or answers. Route records live in each cell's authority store. Cells pull generations, verify signatures, reject older or unsigned ones, apply a generation by compare-and-set on their current generation, and keep a last-known-good generation with an illustrative 15-minute staleness bound. On control-plane loss, cells keep serving last-known-good, new cutovers freeze, and an eligible rollback remains a local compare-and-set to the recorded previous generation with actor, reason and audit. Platform releases roll out cell by cell from a canary cell.

Reuse existing logical roles; no blanket microservice rewrite. Migrate through inventory, manifest validation, eligible read-only shadow with append-only evidence bound to its baseline route and generation, a one-app pilot in one cell, generation-checked cutover, cohort expansion during a dual-run overlap, outcome and cost review, and retirement. Reversal requires current previous-route eligibility, records history, and does not undo data or external effects. At illustrative inputs a six-month overlap moves break-even from month 15 to month 33; ADR-012 records only the no-overlap figures and needs a supersession note for the corrected economics.

The executed new slice is a SQLite metadata controller plus a deterministic TCO worksheet. It does not sign or distribute generations, deploy regional services, execute requests, attest shadow reports, integrate real identity or policy, migrate data or schema, or fence distributed writers. Earlier fixture limits remain. Coverage records describe authored and checked lessons; learner mastery and production readiness remain unassessed. Stage 12 ends the requested sequence; remaining work is capstone assessment and real pilot evidence.
