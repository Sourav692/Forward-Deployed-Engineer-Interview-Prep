Stage 5 · Why bounded queues matter

Scenario: A shared AI provider can finish 600 equal-cost requests/second. Incoming traffic comes from three tenants. This toy model assumes a fixed service rate, no retries, and a starting queue of zero.

Expected results

IncomingCapacityAfter 5 seconds (queue cap 1,200)
1,000 RPS600 RPS1,200 queued; 800 excess requests rejected
500 RPS600 RPS0 queued; no overload rejection

Teaching simplification: results are aggregate fluid approximations. Real requests are discrete, variable duration/token cost, and require fairness, deadlines, and retry control. This lab does not model fairness.
